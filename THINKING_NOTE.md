# Thinking Note

## Problem Understanding

The research question is:

Does buying NIFTY after a sharp fall work?

The main idea is to check whether the NIFTY index gives positive returns after experiencing a significant fall.

## Research Hypothesis

If NIFTY falls sharply, buying after the fall may provide a positive return during the selected holding period.

However, the result may not always be profitable because market conditions can change.

## Clarifications

The research requires three important decisions:

- What percentage fall should be considered a sharp fall?
- How many trading days should the position be held?
- How many years of historical data should be tested?

## Test Approach

The prototype uses sample trading results to demonstrate the research workflow.

The sample includes:

- Total trades: 10
- Profitable trades: 6
- Losing trades: 4
- Win rate: 60%
- Average return: 1.20%

## Learning

The sample shows a positive average return, but it is not enough to prove that the strategy always works.

Real historical data is required for a reliable conclusion.

## Limitations

- The current prototype does not use live market data.
- The displayed results are illustrative.
- Transaction costs and slippage are not included.
- Market conditions are not separately analysed.

## Future Improvements

- Use real NIFTY historical data.
- Calculate actual returns automatically.
- Add charts and performance metrics.
- Include transaction costs.
- Compare the strategy with buy-and-hold.
