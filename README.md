Trading Research AI

Trading Research AI is a simple AI-native trading research prototype that helps users explore and test trading ideas through a structured research workflow.

Research Question

Does buying NIFTY after a sharp fall work?

Research Workflow

The project follows five steps:

1. ASK – Enter a trading research question.
2. CLARIFY – Define the sharp fall, holding period, and test period.
3. DEFINE – Create a clear research hypothesis.
4. TEST – View illustrative sample trading results.
5. LEARN – Understand the findings and plan the next investigation.

Features

- Simple and interactive research interface
- Custom trading research question
- Adjustable fall percentage
- Selectable holding period
- Selectable historical test period
- Sample trading performance results
- Research conclusion and future investigation ideas
- Restartable research workflow
- Responsive and modern dark-themed design

How to Run the Project

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the project folder in Visual Studio Code.
3. Open "index.html" using Live Server.
4. Enter a research question.
5. Follow the research workflow from ASK to LEARN.

Sample Data Disclaimer

The current prototype uses illustrative sample data for demonstration purposes.

The displayed results are not real historical NIFTY market results and should not be used for actual trading or investment decisions.
